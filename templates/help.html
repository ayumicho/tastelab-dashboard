Data Synchronization

All video and sensor data is securely stored on an external platform (MinIO). The dashboard does not hold this data automatically.

To view your latest experiments, you must retrieve the data:

  1. Locate the Sync Data button (usually found in the experiment overview ).
  2. Click the button once.
  3. The system will fetch new data from MinIO and populate the local database.
Note: The system also performs an automatic sync every 24 hours. If you just finished an experiment, use the manual button to see it immediately.

Manual Annotation User Guide

If your experiment involves Computer Vision (Detection & Tracking), you must manually label participants before the system can generate heatmaps or movement data.

1
Start the Job

Go to the Manual Annotation page. Find your experiment under "Pending Jobs" and click "Start Labeling".

2
Set Participant Count (Critical)

A popup will ask for the number of participants. You must enter the exact number of people who were in the room.

Incorrect counts will corrupt the tracking data.

3
Assign Identities

You will see a series of images (crops). Determine who the person is and assign them a number (e.g., Person 1, Person 2).

  • Consistency is key: The person you label as "Person 1" in the first image must be "Person 1" in all subsequent images.
  • Do not group different people into the same ID.
4
Training the Model

Once you finish labeling, the data is sent to our processing pipeline (Dagster). The AI model will train on your inputs to learn who is who.

After you finish manual labeling, the system needs time to process the video and generate the tracking paths.

Processing Time: This process typically takes up to 1/2 hours.

Once the processing is complete, the data will be available in MinIO. You will need to click the Sync Data button again to pull the final tracking results into your dashboard.

Understanding Results

The heatmap visualizes the density of movement in the room based on the manual labeling you performed.

  • Red Areas: High traffic. Participants spent a lot of time here.
  • Green/Blue Areas: Low traffic. Participants passed through quickly.

Use the Timeline Slider at the bottom of the tracking page to replay the movement and see where people were at specific timestamps.

If audio was recorded, the dashboard uses NLP (Natural Language Processing) to determine the emotional tone of the conversation.

  • Positive: Happy, Excited, Grateful.
  • Negative: Angry, Disgusted, Worried.
  • Neutral: Standard conversation.
